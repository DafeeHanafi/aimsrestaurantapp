How to order from the table QR

Use the sticker on your table. Pay at the counter when you leave. Nasi Campur is ordered at the counter, not on this phone menu.

Print this page, or in the browser choose Print → Save as PDF, and send the file to customers or laminate it at the table.

1. Scan the table QR

Point your phone camera at the sticker. The page must show your table number at the top. If it does not, tell staff. Do not order on another table’s code.

2. Add dishes with + and −

Browse Breakfast Sets, Nasi Goreng, Nasi Putih, Fried Noodles, Roti, and Drinks. Tap + for each portion. Tap − to take one away. Two Set A is quantity 2 and is charged twice.

3. Set drinks first

Breakfast sets: pick Kopi or Teh before you add. Other drinks: pick Hot or Cold if both prices are shown. Canned drinks are labelled “Can · no bungkus packing”.

4. Choose Dine-In or Bungkus

At the bottom of the screen. Dine-In is eat here. Bungkus is takeaway. Bungkus adds $0.30 packing per food item and made drink. Canned drinks have no packing fee. You will see an alert when you tap Bungkus.

5. Checkout and send

Tap Checkout tray. Check the list and total. Add a kitchen note if you need one (less spicy, no egg). Tap Send. You should see a kitchen ticket code. Keep that code if staff ask.

6. Pay at the counter

Food goes on your table bill. There is no payment in the app. Call staff if something is wrong. To order more, add again and send another tray — it still goes on the same table.
