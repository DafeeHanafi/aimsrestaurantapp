A. Nasi Campur POS

Open POS. Tap the table number. Choose Dine-In or Bungkus. Set protein portion qty with + / −. Rice is free — do not add a rice line. Tap +50¢ as many times as needed for extra / bigger meat. Ikan has no extra. Veggie and condiments is $1 each, also with +50¢ if needed. You need a protein before Send plate. Bungkus packing is $0.30 × every protein and veggie portion.

B. Guest à la carte

Customer scans the table QR. If they order the wrong table, check the number on screen. Their send creates a kitchen ticket and adds to that table bill. Breakfast drink choice and Hot/Cold must be selected on the card before quantity.

C. Kitchen (KDS)

New tickets land in Preparing. Tap preparing → ready when plated. Tap ready → done when taken. Filter Preparing / Ready / Done / All. Ticket shows qty × item, Dine-In or Bungkus, and guest vs POS.

D. Table bills

Bills combines guest menu + POS for that table. Collect cash / PayNow at the counter (payment is not in the app). Close bill when they have paid. History stays under settled. Closing does not delete sales.

E. Z-report and close day

Z is today in Singapore business date. Dine-In vs Bungkus vs total. Export today CSV if needed. Close day stamps a snapshot. After that the day can show as already closed.

F. Accounts

Accts lists every sold line. Set From / To dates. Export Excel CSV (opens in Excel). Use this for bookkeeping, not the kitchen screen.

G. If something fails

Top-left should say hosted supabase, not connecting. If send fails, check the phone network and try again. Do not mix this site with other Aims apps. PIN, in-app payments, printers, and offline mode are not live yet — write tickets by hand only if the site is down.
